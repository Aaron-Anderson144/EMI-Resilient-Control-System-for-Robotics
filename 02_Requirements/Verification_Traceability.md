# Requirement and failure-mode verification traceability

Reviewed: 2026-09-09. Scope: the v0.3 actuator, Phase 3 numerical prototype and preserved circuit evidence.

This map connects the existing [requirements](System_Requirements.md), [simulation test IDs](Simulation_Test_Plan.md) and [FMEA IDs](Failure_Modes_and_Effects_Analysis.md) to implementation and recorded evidence. It does not approve draft requirements, change FMEA scores, or turn simulation checks into hardware validation. **Covered** means the stated numerical behavior is supported within the recorded fixture. **Partial** identifies remaining subclaims. **Planned** means the required behavior or physical evidence is absent.
