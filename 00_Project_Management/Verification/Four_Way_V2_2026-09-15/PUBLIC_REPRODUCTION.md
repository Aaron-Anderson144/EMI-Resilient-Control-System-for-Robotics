# Public evidence and reproduction scope

The compact checkpoint records the completed study and preserves its original
paths, hashes, acceptance receipt, tables, and audit summaries. It supports
inspection of the reported conditional results.

Reconstructing every audit or regenerating Results.md requires the full retained
development, evaluation, native-circuit, and source-return archives. The reporting
script identified by research_results_sources.json is retained with that external
reporting archive; it is not included in this source publication. The compact
checkpoint and Windows app are not substitutes for those complete archives.

For source dependencies, follow the existing publication reproduction guide and
restore the six hash-pinned inputs before running the scientific workflows.
Generated receiver MEX binaries are excluded from Git; matching Windows binaries
are supplied in the app, while source rebuilds need MATLAB and a supported C++
compiler.

Signal Lab 1.5 includes evaluation and source-return launch controls. Public
downloads leave their local acceptance binding unset. Configure the original
accepted workspace and receipt as described in the Workbench guide; the pinned
source and evidence inventory must still verify. This release does not reissue
the scientific acceptance or relax its conditions.

The completed study met its simulation guards but did not meet the declared
combined-benefit criterion under any of its 16 receiver assumptions. No hardware
validation is claimed.
