# Public announcement draft

The EMI Resilient Control System for Robotics project has reached a new simulation milestone: electrical interference now feeds through a modeled encoder circuit and decoder into closed-loop motor control.

The checkpoint passes all 468 project tests and preserves 116 historical controller records exactly. Its 24 required native circuit comparisons pass numerically, including six documented tighter-tolerance reruns.

The development result also sets the next research question. The lower exposure produces no persistent EMI count error. The stronger exposure exceeds the receiver model's assumed operating domain in every configuration, so the planned comparison of electrical and software mitigation remains unopened.

The new illustrated report explains the project, the causal model, the verified results, and what remains to be established. This is simulation progress; combined mitigation benefit and hardware validation are still open.
